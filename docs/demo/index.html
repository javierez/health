1 · Entrar

Same login as Vesta: better-auth, email and password, 7-day session. Since 18-oct, also «¿Olvidaste tu contraseña?» with a code by SMS.

¿Olvidaste tu contraseña?

  1. On the login, «¿Olvidaste tu contraseña?» → your email → «Enviar código SMS».
  2. An SMS reaches your phone (the 636): «Tu código de verificación de Salud es: 482913. Válido por 5 minutos.»
  3. Type the 6 numbers and the new password → back to the login with «Contraseña cambiada».
Like VestaBetter than Vesta
  • better-auth 1.3.4, same version
  • 7-day session, 5-min encrypted cookie cache; 5 tries a minute
  • 20 s timeout so the button never hangs
  • Reset by a 6-digit SMS code, stored hashed, valid 5 min, used once; asking again kills the old one
  • Same answer whether the email is yours or not
  • New password: 8+, uppercase, lowercase and a number (also in «Cuenta»)
  • A code dies after 5 wrong tries (Vesta: unlimited)
  • A new password signs out every device (Vesta: sessions stay)
  • Send limits live in the database: 3 codes per 15 min to your phone, 5 per IP
  • «Contraseña cambiada» shows on the login (Vesta drops it)
What is different, on purpose
  • No «Regístrate»: one user only, created by scripts/crear-usuario.ts. Google only lets in an email that already exists.
  • No SMS code at every login (Vesta's optional 2FA can be skipped anyway: it runs after the session cookie is set).
  • You give your email, not your phone: the code always goes to the one number on file (WHATSAPP_TO).
  • After login you go back to the page you asked for (?callbackUrl=).
What stays open without a session
  • /login (with its two reset steps) and /api/auth/* (better-auth itself).
  • /api/webhooks/*: Pocket, WHOOP and WhatsApp each carry their own secret.
  • /api/sync: checks its own bearer.
  • Everything else, images included: redirect to /login. An /api/ route answers 401. Checked 18-oct: an action of the panel posted to /login without a session does not run.

2 · Chat

The main screen. Whatever you'd tell a friend who tracks your health.

Three ways in

  • Text: «Escribe o toca el micro».
  • Voice: the mic. It's transcribed and answered like text.
  • Photo or video: the camera. A plate, the scale, the pill, the book, a cream. Up to 6 photos at once; on a computer, also paste (⌘V) or drag them in.

What you see happen

  • One short bubble per reply, with the count riding on it: «Apuntado, sentadilla 4x6 a 100. Gimnasio: 3 de 4.»
  • A photo of a cream or a supplement: it reads the label, saves it (creams in «Piel», supplements in «Comida») and looks up its sheet on the web. Food or a drink, even packaged, is a meal.

7 · Rutinas, vídeo y dibujos 3D 22-oct

Send a training video (one exercise, several, or a whole routine someone gave you). It names each exercise, saves the routine in order, logs what you did, and draws every new exercise once as an animated 3D mannequin.

What happens with a video

  1. Cut for free. ffmpeg takes ~60 frames (one every 0,8 s) and lays them out on 3 contact sheets of 20.
  2. Read once, cheap. One Sonnet call reads the 3 sheets: one line per exercise, with seconds, reps, side and equipment; resting and walking go apart. ~7k tokens in, ~1k out: ~$0,04.
  3. The agent gets text, not 60 images (that was ~$0,30 a turn and ran out of room). It also gets the names you already use, so the next video comes back in your words.
  4. It saves the routine (rutina_entreno: each exercise once, in order) and today's session, linked to the routine. A montage's few seconds are a sample, so it does not copy them as your sets.
  5. Part of a routine («hoy solo los tres primeros, 3 series») logs only those, still linked: the card shows «3 de 7».
  6. Each new exercise is looked up on the web once (how, muscles) and drawn once (gpt-image-1.5): a few cents, after the reply, never again.

10 · Comida new

Everything you eat, food by food. Send a photo or say it («una caña y unas bravas»); the agent estimates the grams and nutrients of each food, or reads them off the label.

  1. Photo or words, by WhatsApp or the web chat: one meal, every food in it (coffee and alcohol too: 60 mg a solo, 8 g a caña). Several photos at once (the plate and its label) are one meal, and a line right after them («esto es la cena») goes with them. On the web you can also paste or drop them.
  2. The reply ends with how far into the day you are: «Hoy: 1.640 kcal, 98 g de proteína de 120». Short at dinner, it names a food that closes the gap («un yogur griego y una pera»).
  3. «Lo de siempre», «lo mismo que ayer», «otro igual»: it copies that meal food by food, without asking.
  4. 40 min before bed, if no dinner was logged, the bedtime message asks what you had.
  5. Wrong? «El arroz eran 100 g»: it deletes the meal and logs it again.

12 · Baño new

Each time you go to the toilet and how it went, told by WhatsApp in your own words. With the urine infection in March and the prostatitis until April, it is the one place where a symptom coming back shows up.

  1. «Acabo de mear y me escuece un poco» → Pis: escozor. «He cagado, normal» → Caca: tipo 4 (the Bristol scale: 1 hard lumps, 4 normal, 7 liquid). «Esta noche me he levantado dos veces a mear» → two pees at 03:00. «He tardado 10 minutos» is kept too.
  2. What it keeps: pee or poo, how it went (bien, regular, mal) and only the symptoms you say: escozor, urgencia, dolor, sangre, no vacía del todo, chorro flojo.
  3. The reply is one line with the day so far: «Apuntado. Hoy 3 pis y 1 caca».
  4. Blood, burning or pain with fever, or burning two days running: a second line telling you to call your doctor.
