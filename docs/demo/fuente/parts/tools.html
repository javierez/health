What it knows before answering
  • Last nights (sleep, need, recovery), today's list, commitments and streaks.
  • The full medical history, «pending» first.
  • What you've eaten today against your protein and fibre target, and the meals you repeat («lo de siempre»).
  • Your creams and supplements, and the evidence behind «Haz» and «Evita» and behind the creams, so its advice cites the same sources as the panel.
