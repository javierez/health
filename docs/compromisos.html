3 · How it decides

What counts · WHOOP + what you tell the chat + Pocket, counted once

WHOOP weightlifting at 18:00 + «gimnasio hecho» at 19:10 = 1 session (3 h window). Gym / run / bike from WHOOP's sport; a ride you only mention is read from the words («bici», «spinning»). Nothing is stored: it's worked out from the data each time.

When it writes · 18:00 to 21:00, at most once a day, only if you're behind

Behind = fewer than the pace (4 a week by Thursday → 1.7). «Every day» = sessions left ≥ days left. Not if you already trained today. The morning note also mentions them, with no extra message.

How you feel · low recovery → something easy, or move it to a day with a free slot

It reads the same WHOOP sleep and recovery as the bedtime note.

Calendar and Gmail · free slots ≥ 75 min until Sunday; unread mail as workload

Read-only. Leaves out all-day events, events marked free and ones you declined. Today's free slots start from now.
